03 — Process Safety & Green Chemistry

A Route Is Not Finished Until It Is Safe and Recoverable

— 01

Process Safety & EHS Compliance

Adherence to Environmental, Health and Safety (EHS) standards, with defined chemical handling protocols applied to every reaction we scale. Hazards are assessed before scale, not after an incident.

— 02

Solvent Recovery by Design

Robust solvent recovery systems mean fresh solvent purchase is a design consideration in the route itself — not an afterthought once the chemistry is fixed.

— 03

Green Chemistry Commitment

Commitment to green chemistry principles, waste reduction and safe environmental practices — the difference between a route that can run for a decade and one that cannot.

World-Class Manufacturing Network

Viva Quest partners with select GMP-certified manufacturing facilities across India to deliver high-purity pharmaceutical chemicals. Our partner facilities are spread over built-up areas exceeding 65,000 sq. ft., featuring state-of-the-art production blocks with advanced reactor capacity.

Designed in accordance with cGMP standards, these facilities include dedicated QC, warehouse, and production areas to ensure the highest quality standards. They are equipped to handle high-pressure reactions up to 20 kg/cm² and temperatures ranging from -20°C to 250°C.

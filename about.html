About Viva Quest Private Limited

Your trusted partner in pharmaceutical chemical sourcing — bridging innovation, quality, and global supply chains.

Business Overview

Our Core Philosophy

Viva Quest Private Limited is a specialized B2B sourcing partner dealing in pharmaceutical chemicals, Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), chemical impurities, KSMs (Key Starting Materials), Intermediates, and CRO services. Headquartered in Hyderabad, India, our team links research labs and manufacturing networks worldwide to supply critical chemical compounds and reference standards.

We are committed to the use of secure and responsible practices pertaining to safety, health, and the environment, in compliance with all regulations in force, international standards, and company discipline.

While we do not own manufacturing facilities, we partner with select GMP-certified manufacturers across India. Our quality team rigorously audits every partner to ensure compliance with international standards. Our in-house analytical laboratory is equipped with advanced instrumentation including HPLC, GC, and UV-Vis spectrophotometry.
